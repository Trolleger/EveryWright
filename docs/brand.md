# Everywright: brand

## Name

Everywright. A "wright" is a builder or maker (shipwright, playwright), so an Everywright builds everything.
Always written as one word with a capital E: Everywright. Never "Every Wright" or "EveryWright".

## Personality

Confident, friendly, straight-talking, local. Like a skilled tradesperson who also happens to be great with tech.
Not corporate, not salesy, no buzzwords like "synergy" or "cutting-edge solutions".

## Voice

- Short sentences. Plain words.
- Talk to the owner as "you".
- Specific beats vague: "live in 7 days", "$500", "we answer our phone".

## Look (starting direction; the user may change it)

- Clean and modern, lots of white space, bold headlines.
- A craft or workshop feel, done in a modern way: think a builder's blueprint, not a cartoon toolbox.
- Colours:
  - Ink (text): near-black, #16181D
  - Background: warm off-white, #FAF8F4
  - Accent: a strong blueprint blue, #2457F5, for buttons and highlights
  - Soft accent: pale blue, #E8EEFF, for highlighted cards and backgrounds
  - Use green only for success messages
- Type: a modern sans-serif. System font stack is fine; one web font for headlines is OK (for example Inter or a similar free font).
- Logo: for now a simple wordmark, "Everywright" in a bold weight. A small mark (for example a simple "E" built from straight lines like a blueprint) can come later.
- Rounded corners (about 12px), subtle borders, minimal shadows.
- Photos: none needed for launch. If used, real and simple, never cheesy stock photos of people in suits.
