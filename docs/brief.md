# Everywright: business brief

## In one line

Everywright gets Toronto small businesses online in 7 days, keeps them on a monthly plan, and grows into their go-to partner for anything digital, done fast with AI.

## Who we are

- Two partners (brothers) in Toronto. One builds, one sells.
- Brand new: no clients or reviews yet.
- We use AI to build fast, and a person checks and stands behind every page.

## Who the site is for

Owners of small local businesses in Toronto: restaurants and cafés, home trades (renovation, plumbing, landscaping), salons and barbers, small clinics and shops.
They are busy, not technical, often reading on a phone, and wary of agencies that are slow, expensive or disappear after launch.

## What the site must do

1. Make the offer clear in five seconds: a new website, live in 7 days, see it before you pay.
2. Show the three packages and prices openly.
3. Build trust without fake proof: be clear, local, honest and specific.
4. Get them to request a free demo.

## The offer

"A new website, live in 7 days. See it before you pay."

- We build a demo of their new site first, free.
- If they like it, they pay 50% to start. If they don't love the final site, they don't pay the balance.
- Every site can come with a monthly care plan so they never have to touch it.

## Packages (CAD, early-client pricing for our first 5 clients)

| Package | What's included | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 5-page mobile-friendly site, contact form, live in 7 days | $500 |
| Growth (most popular) | Starter + monthly care plan + Google Business Profile setup | $800 + $99/month |
| Everything | Growth + logo, brand kit and 10 social media graphics | $1,500 + $99/month |

Add-ons, quoted per job: online booking, local search and Google Ads, AI chat, 3D, music, automations.

Care plan includes: hosting, small edits and updates (menus, prices, hours, photos), Google Business Profile upkeep, and help when something breaks.

## Our edge (use these themes in the copy)

- Demo first: they see their site before paying.
- Speed: live in 7 days, while many agencies take weeks.
- Fair, open pricing.
- Local and reachable: real people in Toronto who reply.
- We stick around: the care plan means we handle updates.
