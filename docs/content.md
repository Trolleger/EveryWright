## FAQ

Heading: Questions

**Is the demo really free?**
Yes. We build a demo of your new site at no cost. If you don't like it, you owe nothing.

**How can you build a site in 7 days?**
We use modern tools, including AI, to handle the slow parts, and we focus on what your business actually needs. A person checks every page before it goes live.

**What do I need to give you?**
Your logo if you have one, a few photos, and your services, menu or prices. We'll handle the rest, including writing the text.

**What does the care plan include?**
Hosting, small updates like new prices, hours or photos, keeping your Google Business Profile up to date, and fixing anything that breaks.

**Do I own my website?**
Yes. Once it's paid for, it's yours.

**What if I already have a website?**
We'll build a demo of a better version. If you like it, we can move your domain and set everything up for you.
